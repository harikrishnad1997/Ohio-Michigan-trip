# Ohio & Michigan NPS Road Trip 🦫🌅

A three-day road-trip itinerary from the Chicago suburbs to Cuyahoga Valley National Park and back — timed around a Park Ranger's own list, with a family night in Michigan and a battlefield stamp on the way home for Thanksgiving.

**🌐 Live site: [harikrishnad1997.github.io/Ohio-Michigan-trip](https://harikrishnad1997.github.io/Ohio-Michigan-trip/)**
**🗺️ Trip map: [harikrishnad1997.github.io/Ohio-Michigan-trip/map.html](https://harikrishnad1997.github.io/Ohio-Michigan-trip/map.html)**

## The trip

**Mon Nov 23 – Wed Nov 25, 2026** · Lombard, IL → Cuyahoga Valley, OH → West Bloomfield, MI → Monroe, MI → home

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Mon Nov 23** | 9:00 AM Enterprise pickup in Lombard → ~351 mi / ~5 hrs 45 mins east on the Ohio Turnpike → arrive the valley ~4:00–4:30 PM ET → optional First Ladies (Canton) exterior-only detour if running early (closed Mondays) → hotel night near the park → early night |
| **Tue Nov 24** | **The ranger's Cuyahoga day, filled out (~9.6 mi hiking):** Beaver Marsh at sunrise (7:24 AM) → Everett Covered Bridge + Furnace Run Loop (1.9 mi) → Boston Mill Visitor Center at 9:30 AM opening for the stamp → Blue Hen Falls (3 mi) → Brandywine Falls + Gorge Loop → Peninsula village for lunch (Lock 29, Trail Mix store) → Deep Lock Quarry (1.4 mi, the flex stop) → Ledges Trail timed for sunset at Ledges Overlook (5:00 PM) → drive ~2 hrs 45 mins to his aunt's in West Bloomfield for the night |
| **Wed Nov 25** | Leave West Bloomfield ~8:30–9:00 AM → **River Raisin National Battlefield Park**, Monroe ~10:00 AM (stamp) → leave ~11:30 AM ET → ~285 mi home → Enterprise Lombard return by ~4:00–4:30 PM CT (branch closes 6:00 PM) → home for Thanksgiving |

## Passport stamps

Two of the five sites are collectable on this trip — the other three are closed or out of season Mon–Wed in late November:

- [ ] **Cuyahoga Valley National Park** — Boston Mill Visitor Center — Tue morning
- [ ] **River Raisin National Battlefield Park** — Visitor Center, Monroe — Wed morning
- [ ] ~~First Ladies NHS~~ — closed Mon–Wed Nov–Apr (Thu–Sat only); exterior-only Monday option
- [ ] ~~James A. Garfield NHS~~ — closed Mon–Wed Nov–Apr (Fri–Sun only)
- [ ] ~~Perry's Victory & International Peace Memorial~~ — visitor center's last 2026 day was Oct 26; plaza/deck closed 2026–27 for rehab

## Bookings

- ✅ **Rental car — BOOKED**: Enterprise, Lombard (920 E Roosevelt Rd), Mon Nov 23 9:00 AM → Wed Nov 25 6:00 PM, Acura Integra or similar, unlimited mileage, $143.42 total. Confirmation number is kept off this public page. The branch closes at 6:00 PM Wed — the return is right at closing; after-hours key-drop is the fallback.
- 🏨 **Hotel — TO BOOK** (Mon night only; Tue night is at his aunt's): Motel 6 Richfield (~$52/night, refundable, 6.6/10, closest to Beaver Marsh) or Econo Lodge Streetsboro (~$63/night, 7.8/10, closer to the Ledges) — both checked on Expedia Nov 10, 2026, quoted for 2 nights, so Mon-only is roughly half.
- 🚆 **Train — skipped on purpose**: the only Tuesday CVSR service is the North Pole Adventure, a kids'/families' Santa ride; the daytime scenic excursions ended Oct 31.
